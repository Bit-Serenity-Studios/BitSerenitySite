Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 15, 2026  ·  Applies to: Cozy Coffee Merge and other Bit Serenity Studios games

The short version: we do not collect, store, or share your personal information, and we run no servers of our own. Your game progress is saved on your device and may sync through your own Apple or Google account. We have no accounts, no ads, and no analytics that track you.

Who we are

Bit Serenity Studios LLC ("we," "us") is a game studio registered in Colorado, United States. This policy explains how our mobile games, including Cozy Coffee Merge (the "Games"), handle information.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information, and we do not operate our own servers. Specifically, our Games:

  • Do not require us to hold an account, name, email address, or phone number for you
  • Do not collect your location
  • Do not access your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Do not transmit your gameplay data to servers we operate

Game progress and your platform account

Your game progress, settings, and preferences are stored on your device. To let you keep your progress across devices and reinstalls, the Games may also save and sync this data through your own Apple or Google account, using platform services such as Apple Game Center, iCloud, Google Play Games, and your device's backup features.

When these services are used:

  • The data is stored in your Apple or Google account, not with us
  • Apple or Google may provide the Games an anonymized player identifier (such as a Game Center or Play Games player ID); we do not link this to your real identity
  • Syncing and backup are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy and controlled by your device and account settings
  • Deleting the app removes local data; synced data in your platform account is managed through your Apple or Google account settings

App stores

You download our Games through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information as part of providing their stores (such as purchase records, crash statistics, and aggregated install data) under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal information from these platforms, only aggregated, anonymous statistics.

In-app purchases

If a Game offers in-app purchases, payment is processed entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or store your payment details. Your purchase history is recorded in your Apple or Google account, which is how purchases are tracked and restored across devices, and is handled by the platform under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

Our Games currently contain no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics. If a future update adds advertising or analytics, we will update this policy first, identify the providers, and describe what they collect and how to opt out, before the update goes live.

Children's privacy

Our Games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. Because our Games collect no personal information from anyone, they do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has somehow provided us personal information, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct, sell, or delete on our side. Data on your device is deleted by uninstalling the app. Data synced to your Apple or Google account (progress, purchases) is managed through your platform account settings. If you have questions about your privacy rights under laws such as GDPR, CCPA, or the Colorado Privacy Act, you can contact us anytime.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change (for example, when advertising or online features launch), we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top of this page before those practices take effect.
