About the studio

Bit Serenity Studios is a small independent game studio based in Grand Junction, Colorado. We build slowly, publish carefully, and try to make games that feel good to open and easy to close.

Our aim is quiet play: small screens, considered pacing, and the kind of short session you can pick up on a break and put down without regret.
