Notes from a small studio

Hello. This is the first note from Bit Serenity Studios, a one-person independent game studio in Grand Junction, Colorado. I made this journal because most studio blogs feel like press releases, and that is not what I want to do here. This page is for occasional notes: the kind of thing you would jot on the back of a receipt after a good day at the workbench.

The studio exists to make small games with patience. Not slow because it is understaffed, but slow because I think most mobile games could stand to be a little quieter. My aim is titles you can open on a break and put down without regret. If a mechanic starts to feel exciting for five minutes and stressful for the next twenty, it goes back to the workbench.

Right now I am building Cozy Coffee Merge, a small merge game with a shared global leaderboard called the Daily Brew. When it is ready, it will ship on the App Store and Google Play. When it isn’t, it stays on the workbench. That is about the pace to expect from this place.

New notes will appear here when there is something worth saying. No schedule, no cadence promises, no push notifications. Thanks for stopping by.
