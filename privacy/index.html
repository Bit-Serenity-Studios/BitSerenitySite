Privacy Policy

What we collect on this website

Nothing. This site is a static set of HTML pages. We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising SDKs, or cookies. We do not run forms, sign-ups, or comment systems on this site. No personal information is collected, stored, or shared by Bit Serenity Studios LLC when you visit these pages.

What our hosting provider sees

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. When your browser requests a page, GitHub’s infrastructure automatically processes standard web request information (your IP address, the requested URL, your user-agent, and the time of the request) in order to serve the page. GitHub’s handling of that data is governed by their privacy statement, available at docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. We do not receive, request, or retain that request data.

If you email us

Emails you send to contact@bitserenitystudios.com are received on email infrastructure operated by Namecheap, Inc. (Private Email). We use the contents of your email only to reply to you and, if relevant, to keep a record of the conversation. We do not use email addresses for marketing without your explicit request.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone through this site.
