About the studio

Bit Serenity Studios is an independent game studio of one, based in Grand Junction, Colorado, on the western slope of the Rockies.

How we work

One person. One game at a time. No investors, no publishing deals, and no growth-at-all-costs playbook. The pace is set by the work, not by a calendar or a roadmap on a wall somewhere. If a mechanic needs another week, it gets another week.

What we make

Small mobile games. The kind you can open on a break and close without feeling like you left something running. Cozy interactions, considered pacing, and mechanics that reward patience over grinding. If a game is exciting for five minutes and stressful for the next twenty, it is not finished yet.

Where we are

Grand Junction sits between the Book Cliffs and the Grand Mesa, near where the Colorado and Gunnison rivers meet. The town moves at its own pace, and the studio takes after it. Most of the work happens early, in the quiet before the day gets loud.
